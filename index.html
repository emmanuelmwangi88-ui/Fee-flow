What FeeFlow does

Everything a bursar does, in one place

From publishing the fee structure to printing a receipt, each step is recorded so any figure can be explained.

Fee structures that follow your school

Primary, junior, senior or both. Build fees per grade and term, copy between grades, and publish. Every published version is kept.

M-Pesa collection

Parents pay by STK Push or paybill. Payments are matched to the right pupil, and unmatched ones are queued for the school to resolve.

Ledger, arrears and receipts

Balances are worked out by the database from the fee structure and confirmed payments, never typed in by hand. Receipts are numbered and printable.

Bursaries, waivers and discounts

Sibling, staff-child and scholarship rules, one-off waivers, and arrears brought forward. Each carries its reason for the audit trail.

Late fees you control

Optional, with a grace period, charged once per pupil per term. Switched off until you turn them on.

Reminders by SMS

Send fee reminders to guardians with outstanding balances, with a record of what was sent, skipped or failed.

Transport and meals

Bus routes and meal plans with their own prices. Assign a pupil and the charge is added, and updated if the price changes.

A parent portal

Parents link their children, see fees and balances, pay, and download receipts. The school approves each link first.

Reports and exports

Outstanding fees, collection summaries and CSV exports for the board, the auditor or the Ministry.

Privacy & security

Children's records deserve care

FeeFlow holds pupils' names and guardians' phone numbers, which is personal data under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Here is how that is handled.

Each school is walled off

Database rules let a school see only its own records, and a parent only their own linked children.

Money can't be edited from a browser

Totals are computed on the server and only confirmed payments reduce a balance. Changes are written to an audit log.

Clear roles and clear terms

The school is the data controller for its pupils' records; FeeFlow processes them on its behalf. Read the Privacy Policy, Terms and Data Protection notice.

Questions

Frequently asked

How does a school get started?

Choose “Register your school”, fill in the school and administrator details, and you can sign in straight away. New schools start on a free trial plan.

How do parents get access?

A parent registers, selects the school, and asks to link a child using the admission number. The school approves the request, so strangers cannot see a child's fees.

Does FeeFlow hold our money?

No. Parents pay into the school's own M-Pesa paybill. FeeFlow records and matches the payments; it does not keep the funds.

Is M-Pesa payment set up automatically?

Each school connects its own M-Pesa (Daraja) credentials in its settings before STK Push works. Until then, parents are shown the paybill and the admission number to pay with.

Does it support CBC?

Yes. Fee structures cover PP1–PP2, Grade 1–6, Junior School (Grade 7–9) and Senior School (Grade 10–12), as well as 8-4-4 classes for schools still running them.

Where is our data stored, and who can see it?

On a managed cloud database with row-level access rules. A school sees only its own data and a parent only their linked children. See the Data Protection notice for details and your rights.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. FeeFlow is built mobile-first and can be installed to the home screen from your browser.
