Data Protection Registration

Last updated 31 October 2026

Action required by each school. FeeFlow provides the software, but the school is the data controller for its own pupils and parents. Under section 18 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 a data controller must be registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) before processing personal data.

Who is who

The school is the data controller. It decides which pupils are recorded, what fee is charged, and who may see a parent's phone number. FeeFlow only processes that data on the school's instructions — we are a data processor.

Where a parent gives their phone number to a school, the parent should deal with that school for access, correction or deletion. We act on the school's written instruction.

What the school must do

  1. Register with the ODPC. File at www.odpc.go.ke, or at a Huduma Centre. Registration is annual.
  2. Tell parents. Publish or hand out a notice naming the school as data controller, what is collected, and why.
  3. Appoint a data protection officer if the school processes at scale, and publish their contact details.
  4. Keep consent on record where the Data Protection Act requires consent — for example for sending an SMS reminder to a guardian's number.
  5. Honour requests for access or correction within 30 days, and report any data breach to the ODPC and to the people affected.

What FeeFlow provides to help

  • The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use a school can link to from its own notice.
  • A record of who changed a fee, a balance or a payment, so the school can show what happened and when.
  • An export of the school's fee and payment records, in a readable format, whenever the school asks.
  • A way for a parent to ask for correction or deletion of their own details.

FeeFlow's own registration

FeeFlow Kenya is registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as a data processor. A school should keep its own certificate; ours is available to any school on request through dpo@feeflow.co.ke.

Data we hold on children

  • Name, admission number, class or grade, gender, guardian link, and the fee items charged.
  • No national ID numbers, biometric data or medical records are required or stored.
  • Where a pupil leaves the school, the school should archive or delete the record — the service will not delete a pupil automatically, because the school needs the fee history.
