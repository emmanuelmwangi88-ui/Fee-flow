Terms of Use

Last updated 1 November 2026

These terms are between the operator of FeeFlow (, "FeeFlow", "we") and the school that registers for the service. A parent or guardian using the service is bound by these terms through their school.

1. What the service is

FeeFlow is a fee-management tool for Kenyan schools. It lets a school set a fee structure, tell parents what is owed, collect by M-Pesa, and produce receipts, statements and collection reports. It is a record-keeping and messaging service.

2. Accounts and roles

  • A school registers itself and its first administrator. Parents create their own accounts and ask to be linked to their children; the school approves each link.
  • Each person is responsible for keeping their password secret and for what is done under their account.
  • Access is limited by role: a school administrator manages the school's own records, a parent sees only their own linked children, and the platform administrator manages schools but cannot see pupils' records or payments.
  • Tell us immediately if you believe an account has been used without permission.

3. Fees, subscription and trial

  • A new school starts on a trial plan at no charge. We will give school administrators reasonable notice before any charge applies or any feature is limited, and no school will be charged without agreeing to a plan.
  • If paid plans are introduced, their price, billing period and cancellation terms will be shown before you accept them.
  • Fee amounts, paybill numbers, bank details and M-Pesa accounts are the school's responsibility. FeeFlow does not hold, receive or transfer school funds: parents pay into the school's own M-Pesa paybill or bank account, and FeeFlow records and matches those payments.

4. Parent payments

  • A payment is made when the parent enters their phone number and approves the prompt on their handset with their M-Pesa PIN. FeeFlow never asks for or stores a PIN.
  • A payment is not complete until Safaricom confirms it. If a confirmation is lost, the payment is held for manual matching and the school is alerted.
  • Refunds and reversals are decided by the school, not by FeeFlow. Where a school corrects a record, the change is kept in the audit log.
  • FeeFlow does not lend money, does not charge interest, and does not act as a bank.

5. The school's responsibilities

  • to give parents accurate information, and to obtain whatever consent the Data Protection Act requires for holding children's data;
  • to keep its M-Pesa and bank details correct and to reconcile its statements;
  • to resolve disputes with parents about the amount owed; and
  • to register as a data controller with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, Kenya.

6. What we do not promise

  • We aim to keep the service available but it may be interrupted for maintenance, or by faults in Safaricom, Supabase or the internet.
  • Figures shown come from what the school entered and what M-Pesa confirmed. The school should check its own statements.
  • We are not liable for loss caused by incorrect fee amounts, wrong admission numbers, or a parent paying on a wrong paybill.

7. Ending the service

A school may close its account at any time. On request we will export the school's fee and payment records in a readable format, and then delete them after the retention period in the Privacy Policy. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms, after telling the administrator where we can.

8. Changes and governing law

We will post changes to these terms on this page and tell school administrators of anything significant. These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya.
