Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Children's data. FeeFlow stores the names, admission numbers, classes and fee balances of pupils, and the names and phone numbers of their parents or guardians. We treat this as sensitive personal data and we collect the minimum we need to run school fee collection.

1. What we collect

  • School records: school name, location, level (primary or secondary), paybill number, phone and address.
  • Staff accounts: the name, work email and role of each person a school authorises — platform administrator, school administrator or parent.
  • Pupil records: name, admission number, class or grade, gender, and any guardian or transport or meal allocation the school enters.
  • Guardian records: name, phone number, and the pupils you are linked to.
  • Payment records: amount, date, M-Pesa receipt number, paybill and account number used, and the fee item paid.
  • Usage data: the pages you open and the actions you take, kept for security and to investigate reconciliation failures.

2. Why we collect it

We use this data for three purposes only:

  1. to run fee collection — publishing a fee structure, accepting M-Pesa payments and showing balances;
  2. to send reminders and receipts that the school, or you, have asked for; and
  3. to keep the service secure and to reconcile payments against the school's bank and M-Pesa statement.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Each school can see only its own records. Access is enforced by row-level security in our database, not only by the app's menus.

  • Safaricom Daraja API — to send an STK push to the phone number you enter and to read the payment confirmation. We send the phone number and the amount, and nothing else.
  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which hosts the data on our behalf under contract.
  • The school — your child's school sees the pupil's name, admission number, class, balance and payment history.
  • Authorities — where the law, a court order or the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner requires it.

We do not share a parent's phone number with any other school or parent.

4. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you may, free of charge:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your data where we are not required by law or by our contract with your school to keep it;
  • object to our processing, or withdraw a consent you previously gave; and
  • lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, Kenya.

Ask through the school office for anything concerning a pupil's record — the school is the controller of its own records and is best placed to confirm a correction. For your own account, write to us using the contact details below and we will answer within 30 days.

5. How long we keep it

  • Pupil and fee records — for the school year, plus the years the school is required to keep financial records (currently seven years).
  • Payment and audit logs — seven years, so reconciliation can be re-checked.
  • Deleted accounts — removed within 30 days, except for audit records the law requires us to keep.

6. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Passwords are never stored — authentication runs through our provider's password hashing. Each school's data is separated at the database level, and every staff action on money is written to an audit log the school can read.

7. Our role: controller and processor

For pupil and guardian records entered by a school, the school is the data controller and FeeFlow acts as its data processor: we process those records only on the school's instructions. For the accounts people create to sign in (name, email, phone, password), FeeFlow is the controller.

8. Children's data

FeeFlow does not offer accounts to children. Pupil records are created by the school, and a parent can see a child's fee details only after the school approves the link. If you believe a child's record is wrong, or should not be linked to you, contact the school or us and we will act on it promptly.

9. Where data is stored

Data is hosted by Supabase on cloud infrastructure outside Kenya (the project is in the AWS eu-west-2 region, London). Transfers outside Kenya are made to a provider bound by a data processing agreement and appropriate safeguards, as the Data Protection Act, 2019 requires. SMS reminders, if a school turns them on, are sent through Africa's Talking, and M-Pesa payments through Safaricom's Daraja service; each receives only what it needs (a phone number and a message or amount).

10. Cookies and local storage

FeeFlow does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores a sign-in session, your light/dark theme and sidebar preferences, and a cached copy of the app so it opens quickly. You can clear these in your browser settings; you will simply be signed out.

11. If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to put people at risk, we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours and tell the affected schools, so they can inform the people concerned.

12. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and tell school administrators about significant changes.
